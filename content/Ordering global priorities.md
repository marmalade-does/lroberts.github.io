Transformative AI is coming soon and should change the priorities that EA's focus on. 
There's two parallel arguments I've heard that go: 

* AI safety must be solved first, because if we fail we will all die and can't work on problem *x*.
* AI safety must be solved first, because if we succeed the AI uplift will enable us to easily solve problem *x*.

Although I think these arguments carry weight, I don't think they are totalising. And 

\1. AI uplift won't solve certain things.
* eg. if they lock-in. (eg political economy things/we loose ability to coordinate whatever.)
	* eg. we will loose co-ordination when the spaceships leave earth

* Don't want to be caught on our back foot if we don't die (aka pls think on the margin.)
	* Is marginal more P(~die) better than marginally better P(other-things good | ~die )
	* And simple models of this say you should allocate 100% to AI safety.
* Things that side-step the tradeoff by boosting both of them are really good
	* eg. pausing-AI -- esp. since it looks like we will get the intelligence-explosion in 2027 after all :/
